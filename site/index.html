Waqt is a tiny Windows tray app that counts down to the next prayer. Set your city once; it works offline from then on.

Everything a prayer clock needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Always visible

The tray badge shows minutes left, turns gold in the last 15, and switches to hours when the next prayer is far. Hover for the name and time.

Accurate, your way

Twelve calculation methods, from Muslim World League to Umm al-Qura, Diyanet and ISNA, plus Standard or Hanafi Asr. Tested against published timetables.

Adhan, if you want it

A notification at every prayer, with the adhan per prayer at your volume. Stop it from the tray in one click.

Ramadan ready

During Ramadan the countdown to Maghrib becomes your iftar countdown, with a Hijri date you can adjust for local moon sighting.

Set once

Search 171,806 places offline, or type your coordinates. Waqt picks your country's usual method for you.

Featherweight

About 13 MB of memory and no measurable CPU while it waits. It wakes once a minute and at each prayer, nothing more.

One small window, opened once.

Private by design

  • No network. Prayer times are computed on your PC. Waqt itself opens no connections.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no ads, no data leaves your machine.
